# Payment demo brand assets

Downloaded 2026-11-16. Logos are displayed unchanged using their original colours
and aspect ratios. They identify the methods illustrated by the frontend demo;
they do not indicate an active payment integration or endorsement.

- `vipps.svg`: official Vipps app icon from the Vipps MobilePay developer site:
  https://developer.vippsmobilepay.com/img/logos/vipps-orange.svg
  Brand resources: https://brand.vippsmobilepay.com/
- `visa.png`: official Visa blue logo from Visa's website:
  https://www.visa.com/vcom-assets/content/dam/visa/header/vectorblue.png
  Brand resources: https://corporate.visa.com/en/about-visa/brand.html
- `mastercard.svg`: unchanged Mastercard brand mark from Wikimedia Commons
  (the official Mastercard download server blocked automated downloads):
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mastercard-logo.svg
  File: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2a/Mastercard-logo.svg
  Brand resources: https://www.mastercard.com/brandcenter/us/en/download-artwork.html

All marks remain the property of their respective owners. Review each owner's
current brand guidance before using this demonstration in a live payment flow.
